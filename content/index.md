This is a collection of all my notes taken during my MS thesis on the mechanobiology of cells, particularly focusing on the mechanosensitive ion channel [[PIEZO1]]. 

My literature survey and research notes are structured across three primary biophysical axes:

* ### [[Adhesion recruitment of Piezo1 and calcium signals/]]
  Exploring how physical forces drive channel localization at focal adhesions and spark downstream chemical signaling.

* ### [[Curvature dependent distribution and activation of Piezo1/]]
  Analyzing how the geometric constraints and bending of the lipid bilayer act as a passive spatial organizer for the channel.

* ### [[Structure-tension gating of Piezo1]]
  Diving into the molecular architecture of the protein blades and how bilayer tension transitions the channel from closed to open states.
